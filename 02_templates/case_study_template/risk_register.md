## Rating Guide

Use a simple 1-5 scale unless the case defines another method.

- Likelihood: 1 = rare, 5 = very likely
- Severity: 1 = minor, 5 = major impact on case credibility
- Rating = likelihood x severity

## Notes

Record any model, documentation, or interpretation risks that need attention before publishing the case.
